Setup

Setting up together

1Go to etothesmith.github.io/generations. The TV taps Host; phones tap Join or scan the QR.
2The first player is the 👑 Captain. Their phone moves the show along, with no host needed.
3📋 Warm-Up: everybody answers 3 quick survey questions on their phone.
4🗳️ Pick Games: tap your top 3. 🥊 Settle a Beef: challenge someone, or tap No beef.
5Teams are made, then Start the Show! Your phone always says what's NOW and NEXT.
