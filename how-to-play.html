The family game show where every generation plays

A live game show about Black culture & history for the whole family. The TV is the stage, and everybody's phone is their buzzer. It's free, with no app to download.

Set up in 2 minutes

Big screenA TV with a web browser, or a laptop hooked up to the TV.
PhonesOne per player. Any phone with a browser works.
InternetWi-Fi or data. Players can even join from another house.
Players2 or more. It's best with every generation in the room.
  1. Open the show on the big screenGo to etothesmith.github.io/generations and tap Host on the Big Screen. Go full screen and turn the sound up. A 4-letter room code and a QR code pop up.
  2. Everybody joins on their phoneScan the QR code, or tap Join on My Phone and type the code. Enter your name and birth year (that sorts you into your generation), then build your avatar. If you're just watching, check Audience. While you wait, answer the Family Survey questions on your phone, because they come back later!
  3. Pick the showChoose Classic Show, Party Night or Trivia Only. Tap ⚙️ Customize the show for teams, answer time (Quick, Relaxed or Grandma-Friendly), themes (Juneteenth, Christmas, Family Reunion) and which games to play.
  4. Make teams and startSplit by generation (Elders, Gen X, Millennials, Gen Z) or randomly into 2 to 4 teams. Tap a player to move them, or tap a team name to rename it. Then hit Start the Show!
  5. Run it from your hand (optional)Tap 📱 Remote on the TV and scan it with the host's phone. You'll get every button, plus the answers privately. Keep that code to yourself!

Show formats

Classic Show

The full game show: the Board, the Remix round with party games mixed in, then the Final Wager.

Party Night

No board, just a long run of laughs and arguments: Settle It!, Who in the Family…?, Top That!, Two Truths & a Lie, Mama Said, Charades and more.

Trivia Only

Straight culture knowledge with no party games. Perfect for a classroom or a competitive crowd.

Round 1: The Board

Six categories, from $100 to $500. There are 22 categories in rotation, including Slang Across the Generations, Classic Black Cinema, Motown & Soul, R&B & Hip-Hop, Black TV Classics, Civil Rights Movement, HBCU Life, Soul Food Kitchen, Church & Gospel, Jazz & Blues, Barbershop & Beauty Shop and Card Check.

  • Buzz in: one player per team is at the podium, and it rotates every question so everybody plays. Wait for BUZZ! If you buzz early, you're locked out for a second.
  • Answer: tap your choice on your phone. Typed-in answers get judged by the host.
  • Steal: miss it, and the other team gets a few seconds to steal.
  • 📞 Family Huddle: each team gets 2 per game. It adds 15 seconds so the whole team can help.
  • 💰 Family Wager: two hidden tiles. Bet what you want, up to your score.
  • Card Check: miss one of these and get your Black card REVOKED on the big screen. 😂
  • 🎡 Sankofa Spin: every 6 tiles, the team in control spins the prize wheel for +200, +300, Double Next, an extra Huddle, Cookout (+100 to every team) or Spin Again.

Party games laughs & arguments

Settle It!

A hot take hits the screen, like "Raisins belong in potato salad." Everybody votes Agree or Disagree. Then one person from each side gets 20 seconds to argue, and everyone votes again. Whichever side flips the most minds wins. +200

Who in the Family…?

"Who's most likely to show up two hours late to the cookout?" Everybody votes for someone (not yourself!). Pick who the room picks to score. The winner gets crowned on the TV. +50 per match

Top That!

"The Wi-Fi password at Grandma's house: ____." Everybody types the funniest answer they can. Answers show with no names, and the room votes. +50 per vote, +100 for the favorite

Two Truths & a Lie

A family member secretly types two true things and one lie about themselves. Everybody else hunts for the lie. +50 if you catch it. The teller's team gets +50 for every person fooled.

Round 3: The Final Wager

Each team's captain gets the category, then wagers privately on their phone: anything up to their score, or up to $500 if they're behind. Then the question drops. The team huddles and the captain types the answer. Answers are revealed lowest score first, the host judges each one, and the champions get crowned. 🏆

Bonuses & awards

  • 🌉 Generation Crossover: answer a question written for another generation (Grandma nails a Gen Z slang question) for +half the value.
  • 🔥 On Fire: 3 right in a row for +50.
  • 🐾 Underdog: if your team is in last and 500+ behind, right answers pay +50%.
  • 📱 Play-Along: folks on the bench answer secretly on their phones for a small team bonus.
  • Reactions: everybody can fire 👏🏾🔥😂🙌🏾 emojis onto the big screen.
  • Awards at the end: MVP, Bridge Builder, Master Thief, The Closer, Family Comedian, Smooth Liar, Most Likely Legend and more.
  • 📺 Season Mode: tap Next Episode for a new game with the same teams. The season standings keep adding up wins all night.

Host tips

  • Next (or the Space bar) moves the show along. C = correct, X = wrong, P = pause, S = skip.
  • 👁 Peek shows you the answer for a couple of seconds. The Remote shows it privately.
  • ± Scores and Undo Last Points settle family debates.
  • If a phone falls asleep, just reopen it. You can also rejoin with the same name to get your seat back.
  • Add your own family trivia with ✏️ Family Questions in the lobby. ("What year did Grandma and Grandpa get married?")
  • There are 700+ questions, and the game deals ones you haven't seen first, so every game night is different.
