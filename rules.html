The family game show where every generation plays

A live game show about Black culture & history for the whole family. The TV is the stage, and everybody's phone is their buzzer. It's free, with no app to download.

Set up in 2 minutes

Big screenA TV with a web browser, or a laptop hooked up to the TV.
PhonesOne per player. Any phone with a browser works.
InternetWi-Fi or data. Players can even join from another house.
Players2 or more. It's best with every generation in the room.
  1. Open the show on the big screenGo to etothesmith.github.io/generations and tap Host on the Big Screen. Go full screen and turn the sound up. A 4-letter room code and a QR code pop up.
  2. Everybody joins on their phoneScan the QR code, or tap Join on My Phone and type the code. Enter your name and birth year (that sorts you into your generation), then build your avatar. If you're just watching, check Audience. While you wait, answer the Family Survey questions on your phone, because they come back later!
  3. Pick the showChoose Classic Show, Party Night, Trivia Only or 🎓 Practice Run (first time? start here). Tap ⚙️ Customize the show for teams, answer time (Quick, Relaxed or Grandma-Friendly), themes (Juneteenth, Christmas, Family Reunion) and which games to play.
  4. Make teams and startUnder How should we split? pick how the generations get mixed: each generation its own team, Old Heads vs. Young Bucks, Grandparents & Grandkids vs. The Parents, Older Crew vs. Younger Crew (split by birth year), Mixed families (every team gets a little of every age) or Random. The choices change with how many generations showed up. Tap a player to move them, or tap a team name to rename it. Then hit Start the Show!
  5. Try the whole show by yourself (optional)Open 🎮 Try the Show Solo (etothesmith.github.io/generations/solo.html). Type your name and the real show starts with you and a practice bot against the family bots (Grandma Pearl, Uncle Ray, Auntie Dee…). Auto-Host runs it, so just play. On a laptop the TV and your phone sit side by side; on a phone, tap 📺 to peek at the TV.
  6. New to the games? (optional)Anybody can open 🎓 Practice on My Own (etothesmith.github.io/generations/learn.html) on their own phone. It walks through every game with practice questions against Grandma Pearl, Uncle Ray and Auntie Dee, and gives you a "Ready for Game Night" diploma. No room code needed.
  7. Run it from your hand (optional)Tap 📱 Remote on the TV and scan it with the host's phone. You'll get every button, plus the answers privately. Keep that code to yourself!

Show formats

Classic Show

The full game show: the Board, the Remix round with party games mixed in, then the Final Wager.

Party Night

No board, just a long run of laughs and arguments: Settle It!, Who in the Family…?, Top That!, Two Truths & a Lie, Mama Said, Charades and more.

Trivia Only

Straight culture knowledge with no party games. Perfect for a classroom or a competitive crowd.

🎓 Practice Run

A short warm-up that walks through every game once with its own easy practice questions, with a quick "here's how this one works" before each. Practice questions never show up in a real game, and practice scores don't count toward a Season.

Round 1: The Board

Six categories, from $100 to $500. There are 22 categories in rotation, including Slang Across the Generations, Classic Black Cinema, Motown & Soul, R&B & Hip-Hop, Black TV Classics, Civil Rights Movement, HBCU Life, Soul Food Kitchen, Church & Gospel, Jazz & Blues, Barbershop & Beauty Shop and Card Check.

  • Buzz in: one player per team is at the podium, and it rotates every question so everybody plays. Wait for BUZZ! If you buzz early, you're locked out for a second.
  • Answer: tap your choice on your phone. Typed-in answers get judged by the host.
  • Steal: miss it, and the other team gets a few seconds to steal.
  • 📞 Family Huddle: each team gets 2 per game. It adds 15 seconds so the whole team can help.
  • 💰 Family Wager: two hidden tiles. Bet what you want, up to your score.
  • Card Check: miss one of these and get your Black card REVOKED on the big screen. 😂
  • 🎡 Sankofa Spin: every 6 tiles, the team in control spins the prize wheel for +200, +300, Double Next, an extra Huddle, Cookout (+100 to every team) or Spin Again.

Party games laughs & arguments

Settle It!

A hot take hits the screen, like "Raisins belong in potato salad." Everybody votes Agree or Disagree. Then one person from each side gets 20 seconds to argue, and everyone votes again. Whichever side flips the most minds wins. +200

Who in the Family…?

"Who's most likely to show up two hours late to the cookout?" Everybody votes for someone (not yourself!). Pick who the room picks to score. The winner gets crowned on the TV. +50 per match

Top That!

"The Wi-Fi password at Grandma's house: ____." Everybody types the funniest answer they can. Answers show with no names, and the room votes. +50 per vote, +100 for the favorite

Two Truths & a Lie

A family member secretly types two true things and one lie about themselves. Everybody else hunts for the lie. +50 if you catch it. The teller's team gets +50 for every person fooled.

🎤

Sing It!

A word drops on the screen, like LOVE, RAIN or GEORGIA. Teams take turns singing a song with that word in it, with 15 seconds on the clock. You have to sing at least a line, and no repeats! The host decides if it counts. Miss, stall or get ruled out and your team is done for that word. The last team singing wins. +100 per song, +200 to the last team standing

🎵

Hum That Tune

One player per team gets a classic song on their phone, from "My Girl" to "Before I Let Go" to "Kill Bill," and has to hum it. No words and no lyrics! The team shouts the title. 45 seconds, +100 each

Round 3: The Final Wager

Each team's captain gets the category, then wagers privately on their phone: anything up to their score, or up to $500 if they're behind. Then the question drops. The team huddles and the captain types the answer. Answers are revealed lowest score first, the host judges each one, and the champions get crowned. 🏆

Bonuses & awards

  • 🌉 Generation Crossover: answer a question written for another generation (Grandma nails a Gen Z slang question) for +half the value.
  • 🔥 On Fire: 3 right in a row for +50.
  • 🐾 Underdog: if your team is in last and 500+ behind, right answers pay +50%.
  • 📱 Play-Along: folks on the bench answer secretly on their phones for a small team bonus.
  • Reactions: everybody can fire 👏🏾🔥😂🙌🏾 emojis onto the big screen.
  • Awards at the end: MVP, Bridge Builder, Master Thief, The Closer, Family Comedian, Smooth Liar, Most Likely Legend, Songbird and more.
  • 📺 Season Mode: tap Next Episode for a new game with the same teams. The season standings keep adding up wins all night.

Not in the house? Play anyway 🏠📱

Cousins across the country, Grandma in another room, the college kid at school: everybody plays the full game from their phone, wherever they are.

  • Join like everyone else: open etothesmith.github.io/generations, tap Join on My Phone, type the 4-letter room code (text it to them!), and check 🏠 I'm playing from somewhere else.
  • Your phone has everything: the scoreboard, the board, the questions and your buzzer. When it's your team's turn you can pick the tile right from your phone.
  • 💬 Family Chat: tap the chat button to talk trash in real time. Messages pop up on the big TV.
  • 🎤 Voice notes: tap the mic, talk up to 30 seconds, tap again to send. It plays out loud on the TV in the house!
  • 📹 Video call: tap Join video call (or Voice only). Your face shows up on the side of the big screen, and the host can tap 📹 Video call on the TV to use the laptop's camera so you can see the whole living room.
  • 📺 Watch the show on a bigger screen: on a laptop or smart TV, open etothesmith.github.io/generations/host.html?watch=CODE (use your room code) to see the live game board, then play on your phone.

Video works best with about 6 people or fewer on camera. If someone on a work or hotel Wi-Fi can't connect to video, they can still chat, send voice notes and play.

🍗 Side game: Invited to the Cookout

No show going yet? Waiting on Uncle to find parking? Open etothesmith.github.io/generations/cookout.html on any phone or laptop (it's also on the home page and in the phone lobby).

  • Pick who you're rolling up as, then run the backyard tablecloth.
  • Catch the food (mac & cheese, ribs, peach cobbler…) and greet the family. Grandma's blessing gives an extra heart.
  • Dodge the uninvited: hop over ants, burnt hot dogs, store-bought cake and raisin potato salad. Wasps and rain clouds can't be hopped, so switch lanes.
  • RSVP Mat: drop it in your lane and anything uninvited that rolls onto it gets bounced. You earn more mats by keeping a streak.
  • Power-ups: the golden Spades card is a shield; the red drink is slow-mo.
  • Controls: swipe or arrow keys to move, swipe up / Space to hop, swipe down / ↓ for a mat. Your best score is saved on your device.

Host tips

  • 🎮 Auto-Host lets the host play too. Turn it on in the lobby (next to the show formats) or any time with 🎮 Auto on the TV. The show moves itself along: it opens each game, moves on after reveals, picks a random tile if a team takes too long, counts Sing It! songs whose titles aren't repeats, and checks typed Final Wager answers. Peek is hidden so nobody sees answers. You can still press Next or ✅/❌ any time to take over.
  • 🤖 Practice bots: short on players? ⚙️ Customize → Add a bot fills a seat with a family bot that buzzes, votes and plays every game.
  • Next (or the Space bar) moves the show along. C = correct, X = wrong, P = pause, S = skip, T = +10 seconds on the clock.
  • 📱 Host Remote: starts in Simple mode (one big button at the bottom for whatever comes next, plus ✅/❌ when you judge). Tap ⚙️ Full for score adjusting and undo. The sound pad plays 👏🏾 Applause, 😮 Ooooh, 🥁 Drumroll, 📢 Airhorn (and more in Full mode) on the TV. ⏱ +10s gives a slow reader more time.
  • Grandma-friendly phones: anybody can tap Aa at the top of their phone for bigger text and buttons. Phones also buzz and flash when it's your turn, and stay awake during the show.
  • 👁 Peek shows you the answer for a couple of seconds. The Remote shows it privately.
  • ± Scores and Undo Last Points settle family debates.
  • If a phone falls asleep, just reopen it. You can also rejoin with the same name to get your seat back.
  • Add your own family trivia with ✏️ Family Questions in the lobby. ("What year did Grandma and Grandpa get married?")
  • There are 910+ questions, and the game deals ones you haven't seen first, so every game night is different.
